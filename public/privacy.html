Privacy

Privacy notice

We only collect what we need to make and deliver your doors, and we never sell your details.

Who looks after your information

Pivot Designs (Tim Flynn, sole trader, 61 Bridge Street, Kington, HR5 3DJ) is responsible for your personal information. Contact: tim@pivotdesigns.co.uk.

What we collect

  • When you send an order request: your name, email address, phone number (if you give it), delivery address, postcode, the doors you've designed and any notes.
  • When you pay: Stripe processes your payment. We receive confirmation of the amount and that it's paid, but never your full card details.
  • When you email us: your email address and whatever you choose to tell us.
  • The door list in the configurator is saved in your own browser so it isn't lost if you leave the page. It isn't sent to us until you send an order request.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

To quote for, make, deliver and support your order, and to email you about it. The legal basis is that we need it to carry out a contract with you, or to take steps you've asked for before one. We also keep order records because the law requires us to for tax and accounting.

We won't send you marketing emails unless you've asked us to.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run the business, each of which protects your information:

  • Render: hosts this website and our order database (servers in Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Stripe: processes payments.
  • Resend: sends our order emails.
  • Our courier: your name, address and phone number, to deliver your doors.
  • Google Fonts: our pages load their typefaces from Google, which receives your device's IP address when a page loads.
  • 123-reg: our email provider, and our accountant for tax records.

Some of these services may process information outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for six years after your order, as tax rules require. Order requests and prices that don't go ahead are archived after 30 days and deleted after two years. We keep them that long so we can re-quote quickly if you come back.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how we use it. Email tim@pivotdesigns.co.uk and we'll reply within one month. If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
