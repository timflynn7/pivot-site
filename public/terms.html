Ordering, delivery, cancellations and refunds

Every Pivot door is cut to your sizes. Here's how ordering works, and what happens if something isn't right.

The short version

  • You send an order request. We check it and email you the confirmed price.
  • Nothing is cut until you've paid, and you can change or cancel freely until then.
  • Because doors are made to your measurements, you can't return them just because you've changed your mind.
  • If a door arrives damaged, faulty or not as ordered, we'll remake it or refund you.

1. Who we are

Pivot Designs is the trading name of Tim Flynn, a sole trader, based at [workshop or business address], United Kingdom. Email tim@pivotdesigns.co.uk. We also sell on Etsy as PivotGB.

These terms apply to orders placed through pivotdesigns.co.uk. Orders placed on Etsy follow our Etsy shop policies. Nothing in these terms affects your legal rights as a consumer.

2. Our doors

Our doors and panels are cut from 18 mm MDF on a CNC router in our UK workshop. They are supplied unfinished (bare MDF), ready for you to prime and paint. MDF edges and machined faces need sealing or priming before painting.

Photos and previews on this website show the design. Your doors are cut to the sizes and options in your confirmed order. Small tool marks on machined surfaces are normal and disappear when primed and painted. Sizes are accurate to ±1 mm.

3. Your measurements

We make every door to the sizes you give us, so please measure carefully. Measure your existing doors, not the cabinet openings, and check how much overlay your hinges need. If you're unsure, email us before ordering and we'll help. We can't accept returns for doors made correctly to the sizes in your order.

4. How ordering works

  1. Order request. You design your doors and send us your order request. This isn't an order yet, and you don't pay anything at this stage.
  2. Confirmed price. We check your doors and email you the confirmed price, including delivery, and a payment link. Prices on the website are estimates until we confirm them.
  3. Payment. Your order is accepted, and our contract is made, when you pay the confirmed price.
  4. Making. We cut your doors after payment. We'll tell you the expected lead time when we confirm the price.

We may decline an order request, for example if a design can't be made at the sizes requested. If we can't make your order after you've paid, we'll refund you in full.

5. Prices and payment

Prices are in pounds sterling. Delivery is included in the confirmed price. We are not VAT registered, so no VAT is charged.

You can pay online by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Stripe, a secure payment provider. We never see or store your card details. You can also pay by bank transfer if we've agreed this with you.

6. Delivery

We deliver to addresses in mainland Great Britain. For other areas, please ask before ordering. We'll email you when your doors are on their way, with tracking details where available. Delivery times are estimates, and we'll keep you updated if anything changes.

Please check your doors when they arrive. If anything is damaged, tell us as soon as you can, ideally within 48 hours, with photos of the door and the packaging. This helps us claim from the courier and get a replacement to you quickly.

7. Changes and cancellations

Before you pay: you can change or cancel your order request at any time, free of charge. Just reply to our email.

After you pay, before cutting: email us straight away. If we haven't started cutting your doors, we'll make the change or cancel and refund you in full.

After cutting has started: your doors are made to your specification, so the 14-day cancellation period that applies to most online purchases doesn't apply to them (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, regulation 28(1)(b)). We can't cancel or refund an order once cutting has started, except where the doors are faulty or not as ordered (see below).

8. Faulty, damaged or incorrect doors

Your doors must be as described, of satisfactory quality and made to your order. If they're not, you're entitled to a remedy under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. In practice, if a door is faulty, damaged in transit, or not made to the sizes and options in your order, we will:

  • remake and send a replacement at no cost to you, or
  • if a replacement isn't possible or doesn't fix the problem, refund you for the affected doors, including delivery.

Please contact us with your order reference and photos before sending anything back. We'll arrange and pay for any return we need. Often we won't need the door back at all.

We can't accept returns for doors that were made correctly to your order, doors that have been painted, cut, drilled or fitted (unless the fault only became apparent afterwards), or damage caused after delivery.

9. Refunds

Refunds go back to the payment method you used, within 14 days of us agreeing the refund. Card refunds can take a few more days to appear on your statement.

We're not responsible for business losses, or for costs of fitting, painting or removal beyond the price of the affected doors, unless the law says otherwise. Nothing here limits our liability where it would be unlawful to do so.
