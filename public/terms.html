Ordering, delivery, cancellations and refunds

Every Pivot door is cut to your sizes. Here's how ordering works, and what happens if something isn't right.

The short version

  • You send an order request. We check it and email you a confirmed quote, which remains valid for 30 days.
  • Nothing is cut until you've paid. Before then you can change or cancel freely.
  • You design your doors, see the price including delivery, and pay online. Customers outside mainland Great Britain send a quote request instead.
  • Nothing is cut until you've paid.
  • Doors are made to your measurements, so they can't be returned because you've changed your mind.
  • Check your doors as soon as they arrive. If one is damaged, faulty or not as ordered, tell us straight away and we'll remake it or refund you.

1. Who we are

Pivot Designs is a small UK business making made-to-measure doors in our workshop in Surrey. Email tim@pivotdesigns.co.uk. Our registered business details are at the end of these terms.

These terms apply to orders placed through pivotdesigns.co.uk. Nothing in these terms affects your legal rights as a consumer.

2. Our doors

Our door fronts and panels are cut from 18 mm MDF on a CNC router in our workshop in Surrey. They are supplied unfinished, ready for you to paint or finish however you like.

  • Paint, handles, hinges and fixings are not included.
  • MDF should be sealed or primed before painting. Light sanding may be needed, depending on your paint system.
  • Small tool marks on machined surfaces are normal and disappear when primed and painted.
  • Sizes are accurate to ±1 mm.

Photos and previews on this website show the design. Your doors are cut to the sizes and options in your confirmed order. We make doors from 300 to 1000 mm wide and 300 to 1200 mm tall; for anything else, please ask first.

Hinge holes: if you ask for them, we drill 35 mm cup holes for standard concealed hinges, centred 22.5 mm from the door edge and 100 mm from the top and bottom (with any extra hinges spaced evenly between), as shown in your door preview. Please check these positions suit your cabinets and hinges before ordering, and tell us in your notes if you need something different.

3. Your measurements

We make every door to the sizes you give us, so please measure carefully. Measure your existing doors, not the cabinet openings, and check how much overlay your hinges need. If you're unsure, email us before ordering and we'll help. We can't accept returns for doors made correctly to the sizes and hinge positions in your order.

4. How ordering works

  1. Order request. You design your doors and send us your order request. This isn't an order yet, and you don't pay anything at this stage.
  2. Confirmed price. We check your doors and email you the confirmed price, including delivery, with a payment link. We'll only change the price shown while you design if your order needs something extra, such as delivery outside mainland Great Britain.
  3. Payment. Your order is accepted, and our contract is made, when you pay the confirmed price.
    1. Design. You design your doors and add them to your basket. The price of each door and the delivery charge are shown as you go.
    2. Payment. You enter your details and pay online. Your order is accepted, and our contract is made, when your payment goes through. We'll email you to confirm.
    3. Making. We cut your doors after payment. Most orders are dispatched within 5–7 working days of payment. We'll tell you if yours will take longer, for example for a large order.

    A confirmed quote remains valid for 30 days. Because material costs change, we may withdraw or change a confirmed price at any time before you pay; after 30 days it lapses, and we'll happily send you an up-to-date price. We may decline an order request, for example if a design can't be made at the sizes requested. If we can't make your order after you've paid, we'll refund you in full.

    Quote requests. If you're outside mainland Great Britain, or you'd rather we checked your order first, you can send a quote request instead. We'll email you a confirmed price, including delivery, with a payment link, and that price remains valid for 30 days. We may decline an order, for example if a design can't be made at the sizes requested. If we can't make your order after you've paid, we'll refund you in full.

    5. Prices and payment

    Prices are in pounds sterling. Delivery is included in the confirmed priceshown before you pay, and included in the total. We are not VAT registered, so no VAT is charged.

    You can pay online by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Stripe, a secure payment provider. We never see or store your card details. You can also pay by bank transfer if we've agreed this with you.

    6. Delivery

    We deliver to addresses in mainland Great Britain. For Northern Ireland, the Scottish Highlands and islands, the Isle of Wight, the Isles of Scilly, the Channel Islands or outside the UK, please get in touch before orderingsend us a quote request instead of paying online and we'll see what we can do.

    We'll email you when your doors are on their way, with tracking details where available. Delivery times are estimates, and we'll keep you updated if anything changes.

    7. Checking your doors on delivery

    Please check your doors as soon as they arrive, before painting, cutting or fitting them. If anything is damaged:

    • if you can, note the damage with the courier when you sign for the delivery;
    • email us within 48 hours of delivery with your order reference and photos of the damage and the packaging;
    • keep the packaging until we've replied.

    This lets us claim from the courier and send you a replacement quickly. We can't accept claims for transport damage on doors that have been painted, cut, drilled or fitted, or for damage that happened after delivery. This doesn't affect your rights for faults that couldn't reasonably have been spotted on delivery (see section 9).

    8. Changes and cancellations

    Before you pay: you can change or cancel your order requesta quote request at any time, free of charge. Just reply to our email.

    After you pay: your doors are made to your specification, so the 14-day cancellation period that applies to most online purchases doesn't apply to them (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, regulation 28(1)(b)). If you need to change or cancel, email us straight away:

    • If we haven't started cutting, we'll do our best to help. If you cancel, we'll refund what you've paid less our reasonable costs already incurred, such as materials bought specially for your order and time spent on it, up to a maximum of 50% of the order price.
    • Once cutting has started, we can't cancel or refund the order, except where the doors are faulty or not as ordered (see section 9).

    9. Faulty, damaged or incorrect doors

    Your doors must be as described, of satisfactory quality and made to your order. If they're not, you're entitled to a remedy under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. In practice, if a door is faulty, damaged in transit (reported as in section 7), or not made to the sizes and options in your order, we will:

    • remake and send a replacement at no cost to you, or
    • if a replacement isn't possible or doesn't fix the problem, refund you for the affected doors, including delivery.

    Please contact us with your order reference and photos before sending anything back. We'll arrange and pay for any return we need. Often we won't need the door back at all.

    We can't accept returns for doors that were made correctly to your order, or for doors that have been painted, cut, drilled or fitted, unless the fault could only be found afterwards.
