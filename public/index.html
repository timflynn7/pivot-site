How it works

From your measurements to your door

  1. Choose a design

    Pick from the range above, or ask us about something different.

  2. Send your sizes

    Measure the existing door, not the cabinet opening, and give us width and height in millimetres.

  3. We cut it to order

    Each door is drawn to your sizes and cut on our CNC router.

  4. Delivered to you

    Packed and sent anywhere in the UK, ready to paint and hang.
