Questions

Good to know before you order

Are the doors painted?

No. Everything is supplied as unfinished MDF, so you can paint or finish it exactly how you want. Seal or prime MDF before painting; light sanding may be needed depending on your paint system.

What's included?

The door front, with hinge holes drilled if you ask for them. Paint, handles, hinges and fixings aren't included.

What sizes can you make?

Any size from 300 to 1000 mm wide and 300 to 1200 mm tall, to the millimetre. Something outside that? Get in touch before ordering and we'll see what's possible.

Can you drill the hinge holes?

Yes. When you design your door, choose which side the hinges go and how many (2 to 4), and we'll drill 35 mm cup holes for standard concealed kitchen hinges, 100 mm from the top and bottom. The preview shows exactly where they'll be. If your hinges need different positions, tell us in the notes.

How do I measure?

Measure your existing doors, not the cabinet openings, and give us the width and height in millimetres. If you're unsure, email us and we'll help.

How long does it take?

Most orders are dispatched within 5–7 working days of payment. We'll always confirm the timing with your price. We'll let you know if a large order will take longer.

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in mainland Great Britain: £12 for the first door and £5 for each extra, free on orders over £400. For Northern Ireland, the islands, the Scottish Highlands, the Isle of Wight or outside the UK, get in touch and we'll see what we can do.

Can I collect my doors?

Yes, from our Surrey workshop, by arrangement and with no delivery charge. Get in touch before ordering, or send a quote request and mention collection in the notes, and we'll arrange a time.

Can I return them?

Because every door is cut to your measurements, we can't take returns for a change of mind. If anything arrives damaged or isn't as ordered, tell us straight away and we'll remake it or refund you. See our terms.
