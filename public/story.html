Our story

I taught myself CNC so my furniture could match my ideas.

I'm Tim, a DIY obsessive who got tired of my carpentry skills not matching my ambitions. Pivot Designs is where that ended up.

I've always been drawn to two things: DIY, and furniture that makes the most of a small space. The frustration was that my skills weren't good enough. I could see exactly what I wanted to build, but I couldn't always make my hands, or my tools, get me there.

That frustration sat in the background for years, until a stint working somewhere deep into technology and automation got me curious about a different way of making things. A CNC router video I stumbled across sparked something. What followed was a long stretch of research, tutorials, trial and error, and slowly upskilling until I had a process I trusted enough to sell.

Pivot is the first step. Right now that means made-to-measure cabinet doors and cupboard fronts, cut with a precision hand tools alone were never going to give me: crisp flutes, clean diamond lattice work, arched grooves matched across a pair of doors, and engraved icons or lettering for anyone who wants something properly personal on their cabinetry.

Everything is cut in my workshop in Surrey, and every order is checked by me before it goes on the machine.

How we work

Cut to order, never from stock

Nothing is cut until I have your sizes. I check every order before it goes on the machine, so if a size looks wrong for a design, I'll ask before cutting. Most orders are dispatched within 5–7 working days of payment.

Doors are supplied as unfinished MDF, ready for priming and painting in whatever colour you want. Because each one is made to your specification, made-to-measure orders can't be returned for a change of mind, so please measure carefully. Read the terms.

What's next

Space-saving furniture and parametric art

Doors are just the beginning. The plan is to move into the furniture that got me into this in the first place: desks and beds that fold away or convert into something else, and parametric art panels for flat-pack furniture. If you want to see where this goes, you're here early.

For trades

We're setting up trade pricing for fitters who want made-to-measure patterned doors without making them. Tell us about your work and we'll keep you posted.
