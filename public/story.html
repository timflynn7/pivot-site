Our story

Precision-cut doors, made to measure in Surrey.

I'm Tim, and Pivot Designs is my CNC workshop. I make decorative cabinet doors and panels, cut to your exact sizes.

I've always been drawn to well-made furniture that makes the most of a space, and to the details that make a plain cupboard look considered. CNC cutting is how I get those details right every time: crisp flutes, even reeds, and arched grooves matched across a pair of doors.

Before Pivot I worked in technology and automation, and I run the workshop the same way: careful set-up, proven designs and repeatable processes. New designs are cut on test boards before they're offered, so the door you order is the door you get.

Pivot starts with made-to-measure cabinet doors and cupboard fronts, for kitchens, utility rooms, wardrobes and bespoke furniture.

Everything is cut in my workshop in Surrey, and every order is checked by me before it goes on the machine.

How we work

Cut to order, never from stock

Nothing is cut until I have your sizes. I check every order before it goes on the machine, so if a size looks wrong for a design, I'll ask before cutting. Most orders are dispatched within 5–7 working days of payment.

Doors are supplied as unfinished MDF, ready for priming and painting in whatever colour you want. Because each one is made to your specification, made-to-measure orders can't be returned for a change of mind, so please measure carefully. Read the terms.

What's next

Space-saving furniture and parametric art

Doors are just the beginning. The plan is to move into the furniture that got me into this in the first place: desks and beds that fold away or convert into something else, and parametric art panels for flat-pack furniture. If you want to see where this goes, you're here early.

For trades

Kitchen fitters, joiners and carpenters

We're setting up trade pricing for fitters who want made-to-measure patterned doors without making them. Tell us about your work and we'll keep you posted.
