Our story

A small workshop making doors that fit.

Pivot Designs cuts decorative cabinet doors and panels from MDF, each one made to order to the sizes our customers give us.

Most kitchens and wardrobes are built from standard carcasses. The doors are what make them yours. Pivot makes patterned fronts cut to fit the units you already have, in sizes off-the-shelf doors don't come in.

Every design starts as a pattern we draw for the router. When an order comes in, the pattern is fitted to that door's exact width and height, so flutes stay evenly spaced and arches stay centred whatever the size. Pairs of doors are drawn together, so the pattern carries across the gap.

We began on Etsy with five designs: fluted, reeded, arched, diamond lattice and engraved icon. Our customers have rated us 5.0, and their feedback is shaping the next designs, including shaker-style doors with fluted and moulded centres.

How we work

Cut to order, never from stock

Nothing is cut until we have your sizes. We check every order before it goes on the machine, so if a size looks wrong for a design we'll ask before cutting.

Doors are supplied in raw MDF, ready for priming and painting. Because each one is made to your specification, made-to-measure orders can't be returned for a change of mind, so please measure carefully.

For trades

Kitchen fitters, joiners and carpenters

We're setting up trade pricing for fitters who want made-to-measure patterned doors without making them. Tell us about your work and we'll keep you posted.
